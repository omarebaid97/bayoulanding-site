Community Financials

This page shows, in plain English, where the community’s money comes from and where it goes. The Board prepared it from the management company’s monthly statements.

Updated October 12, 2026

At a glance

  • $89,850 spent so far this year January through August 2026. The budget for those eight months was $70,245.
  • $42,467 spent on water More than the $42,000 budgeted for the whole year, with four months to go.
  • $19,605 over budget Almost three quarters of that is water ($14,467 over).
  • $2,212.73 per home in 2027 Annual plus water assessment, up from $1,562.38 in 2026. Adopted by the Board; presented to residents on October 15.

Where each dollar goes

Of every dollar the community spent from January through August 2026:

  • Water 47¢ · $42,467
  • Landscaping and irrigation 16¢ · $14,655
  • Trash and recycling 14¢ · $12,905
  • Management company 7¢ · $6,635
  • Insurance 5¢ · $4,056
  • Administrative (postage, printing, records storage, billing) 4¢ · $3,732
  • Other (electricity, drainage fee, tax preparation, legal) 3¢ · $2,714
  • Gate and general repairs 2¢ · $1,686
  • Set aside for reserves 1¢ · $1,000

Rounded; may not add to exactly $1.00.

The reserve is the community’s savings for big-ticket items such as gates, fences, paving and drainage. In past years, reserve money was used to cover operating shortfalls. That is the $75,165 the operating account owes back. The 2027 budget sets aside $11,600 toward rebuilding it.
