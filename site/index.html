Welcome to Bayou Landing

Bayou Landing is a gated community of 68 townhomes on Ann Street and Runnels Street in Houston’s East End, near Guadalupe Plaza. It is governed by Bayou Landing Homeowners Association, Inc., a Texas nonprofit corporation formed in 2003, directed by a volunteer board of three homeowners and administered day to day by Principal Management Group of Houston. This site gathers the things residents look up most often.

New to the neighborhood?

A short orientation for new owners and residents:

  1. Read the frequently asked questions. Assessments, trash collection, gate credentials and resale inspections are all covered there.
  2. Learn the parking rules before your first night here. Common-area parking requires a displayed permit.
  3. Get approval before you change anything outside. Fences, paint colors, patios, doors, lighting and similar exterior work all need written approval in advance — see the Architectural Control Guidelines and the modification request process.
  4. Create your TownSq account. Official notices, meeting announcements and billing all run through it.
  5. Submit the gate access request. Credentials are issued per household after the form is received.
