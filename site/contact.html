Which contact do I need?

  • Gate credentials — start with the gate access request form, not an email.
  • A gate, light or common-area item that is broken — community manager, or a TownSq request.
  • Billing, statements, payment methods — management, through TownSq.
  • Parking permits or a towing question — management; see the parking page first.
  • Approval for exterior work — management, in writing; see the modification request process.
  • Resale certificates and disclosure packages — management.
  • Board policy, elections, community suggestions — the Board.
  • An emergency, a crime in progress, or a medical situation — call 911 first. Notify management afterward.
