News & Notices

A short, plain-English summary of what’s current at Bayou Landing. Official notices are always delivered through TownSq — this page just collects the highlights in one place.

Posted September 23, 2026

Upcoming: Board & homeowner meeting, Thursday, October 15, 2026

The Board is holding a combined board and homeowner meeting on Thursday, October 15, 2026. Time and location will be published in the TownSq notice.

Agenda topics include:

  • the 2027 budget and assessments;
  • a new landscaping schedule;
  • planning the community mulch party.

All owners are encouraged to attend. See the meetings page for background on how Bayou Landing meetings work.

Posted September 23, 2026

Community mulch party, Sunday, November 8, 2026

The HOA is buying mulch, and residents are invited to help spread it around the community. Details on time and where to meet will follow on TownSq.

Updated September 2026

Water usage

  • The community’s water bill has been running well above budget.
  • The City has told us the master meter reader is malfunctioning, and recent bills have been estimated rather than based on an actual reading.
  • The Board and Principal Management Group are working with the City to get this resolved.

Residents can help: please report running toilets, leaks, and any outside faucet that is left running or appears tampered with. Report these to TownSq or the community manager — see the contact page.

Landscaping

Landscaping service has moved to a bi-weekly schedule. Mulch goes down in November, timed with the community mulch party above.

Gates

  • There have been recent app outages and damage to one of the gates; repairs are being coordinated.
  • Report gate problems through TownSq.
  • Close gates behind you, and don’t let someone you don’t know follow you in. If someone suspicious is inside the gates, call the Harris County Pct. 6 Constable non-emergency line at 713-274-3400. In an emergency, call 911.

See the gate access page for how to open the gates, including by text.

Trash

Trash is picked up Tuesdays and Fridays; recycling is picked up Fridays.

Overflowing dumpsters near the Lofts have been attracting problems. If you see overflow or illegal dumping, report it to Houston 311 — call 311, or use the Houston 311 app or website.

This page is a convenience summary. Official notices, dates and details are always published through TownSq; where they differ, TownSq controls.
